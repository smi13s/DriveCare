DRIVECARE WEBSITE
=================
Open index.html in a web browser. For hosting, upload index.html, styles.css, and script.js to the same directory on your web host (Netlify, Vercel, or a conventional website host).

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Edit BUSINESS_SMS_NUMBER near the top of script.js to your actual business phone number (digits only). If left blank, the quote button copies text instead of opening an SMS conversation.
2. Confirm plan prices, oil capacity limits, service details, and service availability.
3. Add real business phone, address, hours, booking/payment links, and your business legal identity.
4. Obtain appropriate review of California repair estimates, subscriptions, automatic renewal, cancellation and privacy terms before accepting payments.
5. This is a front-end website; no database, real payment processing, booking system, or customer data storage is included.
